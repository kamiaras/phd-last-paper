# Contribution and related-work review

Reviewed 24 September 2026, including a follow-up check of four originals supplied by the user. The revised sections are in `fresh_rewrite.tex`; publication metadata changes are in `ref.bib`.

## Scope and main corrections

The contribution claims were checked against the current policy definitions, theorem statements, and the supporting approximation, stability, and lower-bound arguments. This was a review of the claims and their presentation, not a complete independent audit of every proof. All 19 references cited in Related Work were checked against available primary evidence. Full-text access varied; limitations are recorded below.

- Reorganized the contribution section around four results: feasible SDAC policies; online feasibility and regret; infinite-memory and fixed-fraction benchmarks; and the minimax lower bound. Added direct references to the supporting results.
- Qualified the asymptotic upper bounds by fixing the retention coefficient and cost Lipschitz constants. Restricted the claim of an optimal square-root horizon rate to fixed memory horizon and model constants.
- Described the truncation error as a uniform **cumulative-cost** error, proportional to the horizon length as well as the geometric memory factor.
- Replaced the introduction's unsupported description of “dynamic corrective coefficients” with the actual powers of the retention coefficient in the fixed filter. Clipping belongs to the online controller.
- Acknowledged that Golowich et al. already combine simplex-structured DAC parameters and entropy-regularized mirror descent. The claimed contribution is the storage-specific policy construction and analysis.
- Corrected the incomplete comparison with Liu et al.: their paper also treats static state–action constraints and discusses a projection variant with zero static affine-constraint violation.
- Corrected the incomplete comparison with Yu and Neely: their results extend beyond i.i.d. system states and include a fixed-action comparison under a mean-energy constraint.
- Removed the unproved suggestion that SDAC contains general recharge-reset or look-ahead allocation policies. Exact inclusion is claimed only for feasible fixed-fraction policies under this manuscript's dynamics.
- Qualified Khojastepour and Sabharwal's uniform-filter optimality by its time-invariant policy class and acknowledged their time-varying robust scheduler.
- Verified Lin's current-arrival/current-value-function observation before allocation directly in the supplied final 2025 article. Specified the value-function assumptions and the physical constraints shared by its online controller and offline benchmark.
- Replaced the Tassiulas–Ephremides 1990 conference citation with the supplied 1992 journal version. The earlier conference entry was a different publication, not an incorrect year for the supplied PDF.
- Kept the Moran description limited to the random-inflow/prescribed-release claim supported by the available contemporary primary evidence; the original 1954 article remains unavailable.

## Citation-by-citation evidence

1. **Agarwal et al. (2019), `agarwal2019online`.** Verified from the [full PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/agarwal19c/agarwal19c.pdf): Definition 3.3 gives strongly stable linear controllers; Definition 4.1 defines DAC; Theorem 5.1 establishes the cited regret rate. The [publication record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/agarwal19c.html) matches the bibliography. The existing characterization was retained.

2. **Golowich et al. (2024), `golowich2024population`.** Verified from the [full author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01799v2), Definition 3, Section 3, and Appendix C.1. The state is a probability vector and the action domain is independent of the current state. Their DAC parameter domain has simplex structure, and they already use entropy-regularized mirror descent. Added that methodological precedent. The [published record](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/ca4f6e86453e4b117dd3263792053cf5-Abstract-Conference.html) agrees with the citation.

3. **Li et al. (2021), `li2021onlineConstrained`.** Verified from the [full AAAI paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/17035/16842), equation (2), Assumptions 1 and 3, Algorithm 1, and Theorem 1. Their formulation uses separate fixed affine state and action constraints and projected updates on a tightened policy set. The guarantees assume a strictly safe linear controller; the regret model uses bounded i.i.d. disturbances, while safety covers all admissible bounded disturbances. The revision describes their actual formulation without implying that affine constraints cannot encode state–action coupling.

4. **Liu et al. (2023), `liu2023constrainedControl`.** Verified from the [full PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/liu23at/liu23at.pdf), Section 2, Table 1 and its footnote, Remark 3.2, and Theorems 3.1/3.3. Their constraints may jointly depend on state and action and may be adversarial or static. Their main algorithms bound violations, but a projection variant can enforce static affine constraints without violation. The revised comparison explicitly acknowledges this, since the manuscript's storage constraint is itself static and affine.

5. **Kumar et al. (2023), `kumar2023unbounded`.** The [published supplement](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/531230cfac80c65017ad0f85d3031edc-Supplemental-Conference.pdf), Theorem D.2, establishes the stated inverse-discount-gap lower bound for discounted infinite memory with a Euclidean history norm and discount factor at least one half. The revision identifies the norm specialization; the analogy is a comparison of scaling, not a reduction proving the storage result. Replaced the 2024 arXiv-revision citation with the [NeurIPS 2023 publication](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/531230cfac80c65017ad0f85d3031edc-Abstract-Conference.html), volume 36, pages 26229–26270.

6. **Shaviv and Özgür (2016), `shaviv2016universally`.** Verified from the [full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.00353), Section V and Theorem 2. Fixed-fraction power allocation under i.i.d. arrivals has constant additive and multiplicative throughput guarantees. The revised sentence names the guarantee rather than only the policy form.

7. **Arafa et al. (2018), `arafa2018online`.** Verified from the [author-hosted journal paper](https://webpages.charlotte.edu/aarafa/twc18.pdf), Section II and equations (10)–(16). The utility is increasing and concave; Bernoulli full recharges yield an allocation problem between recharges. Added the increasing-utility condition. The bibliography agrees with the article.

8. **Zibaeenejad and Chen (2019), `zibaeenejad2019lookahead`.** Verified from the [full extended paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.12281), equations (8)–(9), Algorithm 1, and Theorem 1. The decreasing sequence applies while no recharge is visible; once one enters the look-ahead window, remaining energy is allocated uniformly until it. The revision states both regimes. Corrected the proceedings DOI to `10.1109/ISIT.2019.8849649` and added pages 116–120; retained the extended-paper link. The [authors' subsequent journal paper](https://www.ece.mcmaster.ca/~junchen/On_Optimal_Power_Control_for_Energy_Harvesting_Communications_With_Lookahead.pdf) corroborates the conference publication.

9. **Yu and Neely (2019), `Yu2019`.** Verified from the [full journal paper](https://eeyuhao.github.io/journal/EnergyHarvesting-TON-2019.pdf), Sections II and V, Theorem 4, and equation (41). The main stochastic model is supplemented by a non-i.i.d. wireless-state extension, with independent bounded energy arrivals of constant mean and the specified independence from past information. The comparator in the extension is a fixed action satisfying a mean-energy constraint. The objective does not directly depend on battery state. The revised description includes the extension.

10. **Asgari and Neely (2020), `10.1145/3428337`.** Verified from the [author-hosted final paper](https://ee.usc.edu/stochastic-nets/docs/energy-harvest-bregman-sigmetrics2021.pdf), Section 2, equations (34)–(35), Theorem 5.3, and Section 5.4. The expected-regret rate is `O(sqrt(T log n))` with `O(sqrt T)` battery capacity; the revised `O(sqrt T)` statement explicitly fixes action dimension. The comparator satisfies a mean-energy constraint. The current arrival is known before allocation and the loss is revealed afterward; the manuscript does not imply that this paper shares its action-before-arrival timing. Removed the optional November month field, which conflicted with the December issue-date field.

11. **Lin et al. (2025), `lin2025optimal`.** Verified from the [supplied final journal article](<C:/Users/Kamiar/Downloads/Optimal_Algorithms_for_Online_Age-of-Information_Optimization_in_Energy_Harvesting_Systems.pdf>). Section III-A1 (printed p. 3149/PDF p. 4) specifies a finite, initially full battery, an allocation-rate limit, and immediate use of current-epoch harvested energy. Section III-A2 (p. 3150/PDF p. 5) assumes increasing concave value functions, zero value at zero allocation, and derivative bounds `0 < L <= g'_t <= U`. Equations (3)–(6) impose the same energy, capacity, and rate constraints on the offline benchmark. Section III-C (p. 3151/PDF p. 6), Algorithm 1, and equations (8)–(11) confirm that the current arrival and value function are observed before allocation. 12. **Tassiulas and Ephremides (1992), `tassiulas1992stability`.** Verified against the [supplied journal article](<C:/Users/Kamiar/Downloads/Stability_properties_of_constrained_queueing_systems_and_scheduling_policies_for_maximum_throughput_in_multihop_radio_networks.pdf>). Section III-B constructs the scheduling policy. Theorem 3.2 (p. 1940/PDF p. 5) characterizes the stability region up to boundary points and establishes the throughput result. Updated the bibliography and citation key to the supplied journal version: volume 37, issue 12, pp. 1936–1948, 1992, DOI `10.1109/9.182479`.

13. **Tassiulas and Ephremides (1993), `tassiulas1993dynamic`.** Verified against the [supplied publisher full text](<C:/Users/Kamiar/Downloads/Dynamic_server_allocation_to_parallel_queues_with_randomly_varying_connectivity.pdf>). 16. **Khojastepour and Sabharwal (2004), `khojastepour2004delay`.** Verified against the [supplied full proceedings paper](<C:/Users/Kamiar/Downloads/Delay-constrained_scheduling_power_efficiency_filter_design_and_bounds.pdf>).
